Are the plots RERA approved? +

Yes. Industrial plots at Hero Industrial Park – 3, Roorkee are RERA approved. Contact our sales team for the RERA registration number and documentation.

Is this project SIDA approved? +

Yes. Hero Industrial Park – 3 is SIDA (State Industrial Development Authority) approved, ensuring legal compliance and government-backed infrastructure support.

Where exactly is the project located? +

Roorkee, Haridwar district, Uttarakhand — with excellent connectivity to NH-58, Haridwar, Dehradun, Delhi NCR and key industrial zones.

What plot sizes are available? +

Multiple plot sizes for manufacturing, warehousing, logistics and industrial investment. Contact sales@gondalinfra.com for current availability and pricing.

How do I visit or get directions to the site? +

Scan the QR code in the Location section above, or submit an enquiry and our team will arrange a guided site visit from Roorkee or Haridwar.
