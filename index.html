06 / FREQUENTLY ASKED

Common
questions.

Are the industrial plots RERA approved?

Yes. Industrial plots at Hero Industrial Park – 3, Roorkee are RERA approved. Contact our sales team for RERA registration number and details.

Is this project SIDA approved?

Yes. Hero Industrial Park – 3 is SIDA (State Industrial Development Authority) approved, ensuring legal compliance and government-backed infrastructure support.

Where are the industrial plots located?

The project is located in Roorkee, Haridwar district, Uttarakhand — with excellent connectivity to NH-58, Haridwar, Dehradun, Delhi NCR and key industrial zones.

What sizes of industrial plots are available?

Multiple plot sizes are available to suit manufacturing, warehousing, logistics and industrial investment needs. Contact us at sales@gonndalinfra.com for current availability.

How do I get directions to the site?

Scan the QR code in the Location section above or contact our sales team for a guided site visit from Roorkee or Haridwar.
