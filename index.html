WHY HERO INDUSTRIAL PARK–3 ?

Hero Industrial Park–3 is a RERA Approved, SIDA Approved & CLU Approved industrial project located on NH-334, Roorkee Bypass, Uttarakhand. Designed for Factories, Warehouses, Logistics & Manufacturing, the project offers a secure and business-ready environment with CCTV surveillance. With up to 40% Government Subsidy, excellent connectivity and essential approvals, Hero Industrial Park–3 offers a smart opportunity to establish, expand and grow your business.
