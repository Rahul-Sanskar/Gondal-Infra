Privacy Policy

GONDAL INFRA PRIVATE LIMITED is committed to protecting the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard personal and business information when you use our services. By accessing and using GONDAL INFRA PRIVATE LIMITED services, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

Information Collection and Use

We collect information from users when they register to use our services or provide information during specific instances. This information includes names, email addresses, business details, and other personal information. Once registered, you are no longer anonymous to GONDAL INFRA PRIVATE LIMITED, and we may publish the provided business and personal details across our platforms, including but not limited to business directories and catalogs, to enhance your visibility and business opportunities.

When you share personal information on our network, you are sharing it solely with GONDAL INFRA PRIVATE LIMITED, unless stated otherwise explicitly. Promotions on our network may be co-sponsored with other companies, and data collected during these promotions may be shared with the sponsors, with prior notification to users. You can choose not to participate in such promotions to avoid data sharing.

We also collect usage statistics, such as pages viewed, unique visitors, browser software, and screen resolution, for analysis to improve user experience and services.

Purpose of Information Collection

GONDAL INFRA PRIVATE LIMITED collects personal and business information to provide a customized experience on our platform. This includes personalized services, interactive communications, and other services, most of which are free. Business information is used to display your business listing and product offerings across our network, maximizing business opportunities for you.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

In order to provide free services, GONDAL INFRA PRIVATE LIMITED displays advertisements based on site usage statistics and visitor trends. We may use your personal and business information to serve targeted advertisements through our websites, newsletters, and customized mailers.

Disclosure of Information

GONDAL INFRA PRIVATE LIMITED will not disclose or share any user's personally identifiable information unless explicitly permitted by the user or under special circumstances, such as complying with the law, protecting our rights or property, or when necessary for maintenance, improvement, or service purposes.

Emails and Direct Mails

We may send emails about our products and services to our users. Additionally, we may occasionally send direct mails about third-party products and services that we believe may be of interest to you. Only GONDAL INFRA PRIVATE LIMITED or its agents under confidentiality agreements will send these direct mailings.

Editing and Deleting Account Information

Users can edit their account information and profile by logging onto our website or by sending a request to sales@gondalinfra.com. It may take up to 30 days for the requested changes to appear online due to verification processes and server cache policies. Deleting or deactivating an account will result in no access to members-only areas, though residual information may remain in our archive records.

Data Security

GONDAL INFRA PRIVATE LIMITED takes necessary steps, within its commercial viability and necessity, to secure user information. While we strive to protect your personal and business information, we cannot guarantee 100% data transmission security over the internet. Users must be aware that any personal or business information posted online and accessible to the public may lead to unsolicited messages from other parties.

Updates to the Privacy Policy

GONDAL INFRA PRIVATE LIMITED reserves the right to update and modify this Privacy Policy as needed. We will inform users of significant changes by posting the revised policy on our website or through other appropriate means. Users are encouraged to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how their information is being protected and used.
